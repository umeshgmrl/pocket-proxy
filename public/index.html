ONE-TIME SETUP
A small detour for your requests.
Route compatible apps through Pocket Proxy, then trust your personal
certificate to inspect and mock HTTPS.

  
01
Trust your HTTPS certificate
Pocket Proxy creates a unique certificate on this Mac. Trusting it lets the proxy read and mock HTTPS traffic routed through it.
PERSONAL CERTIFICATE
Loading…
  1. Add to the login keychain if prompted. If already installed, select its existing keychain.
  2. Search for Pocket Proxy Personal CA and double-click it.
  3. Expand Trust. Set Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) to Always Trust.
  4. Close the certificate window and authorize macOS to save.
Opening or importing alone does not enable trust. The private key stays in the project’s .data folder.
To remove trust later, delete “Pocket Proxy Personal CA” from its System or login keychain in Keychain Access.

  
02
Connect your Mac
Choose the network services to route through the local proxy. Their previous HTTP and HTTPS proxy settings are saved for restoration.
macOS may ask for administrator authorization. Stopping capture or quitting restores saved settings. Select newly added network services here after reconnecting.

  
Connecting a terminal or a specific app?
Some clients ignore macOS proxy settings. Configure their proxy and certificate explicitly.

  
Coverage: HTTP/HTTPS in apps that use the proxy. Certificate-pinned apps and HTTP/3 may need application-specific configuration. WebSockets pass through; their frames are not inspected. Existing connections may need a browser or app restart.
